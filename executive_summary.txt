PREDICTION MODEL
================
Models Trained : Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting
Model Selected : Logistic Regression
ROC-AUC Score : 0.567
F1-Score : 0.471
Encoding Used : Smoothed target encoding for collegeName, city, and state.
  One-hot encoding for registerType and category.
Features Used : groupSize_capped, isGroup, hasCompany, registerType,
  category, collegeName_target, city_target, state_target
Train / Test Split : 80% training  /  20% testing  (stratified)

Model Interpretation : Out of 100 random pairs of one attendee and one
  no-show, the model correctly identifies which is which 57 times. Better
  than random guessing but not a perfect predictor.

Model Limitation : The data ceiling limits accuracy. Future editions should
  collect registration timestamp, distance from venue, communication
  channel, reminder response, and prior IOH attendance history to
  significantly improve the model.


RECOMMENDATIONS
===============
1. Priority : Follow up directly with Chennai Institute of Technology and
  Sathyabama - 171 registrations at below 32% conversion is recoverable.

2. Segment Focus : Target INSTITUTE registrations with dedicated outreach -
  72.6% conversion makes them the most reliable attendees.

3. Registration Push : Promote group registrations over individual ones -
  44.3% vs 36.1% conversion speaks for itself.

4. Karnataka : Do not just push more registrations there. Rethink the
  messaging and channel entirely. 29.6% conversion means the current
  approach is not working.

5. Replicate Success : Identify what was done differently for University of
  Madras and Anna University and apply it to underperforming colleges.

6. Data Collection : Next year collect registration timestamp, distance from
  venue, reminder open rates, and prior attendance history. This will push
  model accuracy well beyond its current ceiling.
